# LinkedIn post

LinkedIn is not an entry platform for the overseas route. The contest says overseas entries go
on **X or YouTube** (see `5_RULES/OFFICIAL_RULES.md`). This post is a showcase of the work and
does not need the contest link (the user's choice, 2026-10-28). If you do add the link to the
X or YouTube entry, the views and likes there are the ones that count.

## Showcase version (no contest link needed)
```
I trained a robot to dance, on a laptop with no GPU. 🦀

A few days ago I picked up KingKong Tech's open-source Jumper crab robot toolkit. Here is what I built on top of it, all in simulation:

• Trained the dance with reinforcement learning (PPO in MuJoCo) on a Ryzen 5 laptop CPU: 256 simulated robots, 1,500 iterations, 2 hours 31 minutes. The reward went from about -2 to 57.
• Designed a neon "Crab Rave" disco floor with the toolkit's design repo, and checked that the robot spawns and the scene loads.
• Gave the robot a black, white and violet striped look.
• Set up reproducible scripts and notes so anyone can rebuild it.

The dance itself is the demo choreography that ships with the toolkit. The scene, the skin, the training run and the filming are mine.

What I took from it: open source and a free afternoon can get you a working robot-learning pipeline. You do not need a GPU cluster to start.

Toolkit: https://github.com/KingKongRobotics/jumper

#Robotics #ReinforcementLearning #OpenSource #MuJoCo #Python
```
