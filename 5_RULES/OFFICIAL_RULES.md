## What the contest is
- Name: Crab Robot Open-source Challenge (Chinese: 螃蟹机器人开源动作大赛). Page title:
  "The viral crab robot is now open source. 100 robots to give away!"
- Run through the BE UNLIMITED community site. Organizer: KingKong Tech (金钢科技), with Douyin
  Technology listed as a co-host in Chinese news reports. Initiated by 梁不歪 (Liang Zheng).
- Idea: use KingKong's open-source toolkit to change Jumper's look, create moves, or add
  intelligence. Start in simulation; the best creations may also be recreated on real robots by
  KingKong.
- The toolkit and its repositories: https://github.com/KingKongRobotics/jumper (training) and
  https://github.com/KingKongRobotics/jumper-design (looks, scenes). The page's example prompt is
  to ask an AI assistant to read the repo and apply a skin such as "Warm Sand Ranger".

## Timeline
- Whole contest: 2026-09-30 to 2026-11-30.
- **Round 1: one month. First-round entries close on 2026-10-30.** The time zone is not stated.
- 3 to 4 selection rounds in total. Round 2 is "coming later", details announced separately.
- The page says entering earlier gives more chances to win.
- Announcement period after selection: 3 to 5 business days, during which eligible objections may
  be submitted.

## Judging
- Weight: 60 % audience response and 40 % expert review.
- Dimensions: appearance (beautiful), motion (playful), skills (useful). Excellence in any one
  dimension is enough; strength across dimensions earns extra credit.

## Prizes and delivery
- 100 robots. "One hundred outstanding open-source projects will be selected to receive a Jumper
  crab robot" and entries are collected across platforms.
- The robots are engineering units, not finished products (Chinese news report).
- Winners lists: kingkong.tech. International announcements and featured works: the official
  KingKong Robotics accounts. China: the KingKong Tech Douyin account and @梁不歪.
- Winners are contacted through the official account inbox. **If you cannot be reached within
  5 days of the official message, the prize is forfeited.**

## Conditions (the "What should I know before entering?" FAQ)
- **Originality:** entries must be original and must not infringe third-party intellectual
  property. Verified plagiarism or infringement leads to disqualification.
- **Music:** no music pack is provided. Entrants must secure their own licenses and take
  responsibility for any music-related dispute or loss. (A silent video avoids this entirely.)
- **License you grant by submitting:** KingKong Tech and event partners receive a perpetual,
  worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable right to use, edit, develop and
  commercially use the entry, with creator attribution kept.
- **Privacy:** entries go through the social platforms; the site collects no entry data. If
  contact or delivery details are needed after a win, the organizer explains the purpose and
  gets consent first.
- **Minors:** may take part only with an adult guardian, who accepts all terms and handles prize
  delivery documents.
- **Open-source license:** entrants must understand and follow the license of the KingKong
  toolkit. Whether an entry must be open sourced depends on those terms. (The code is Apache-2.0,
  which does not require you to open source your own work.)
- **Platform rules:** follow the rules of the platform where you publish.
- **Interpretation:** KingKong keeps final interpretation rights to the extent the law allows.
